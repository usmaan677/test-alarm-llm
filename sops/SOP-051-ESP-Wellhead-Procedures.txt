STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
SAGD Well Pad — ESP and Wellhead Equipment
Emergency Shutdown and Alarm Response
Document: SOP-051  |  Revision: 3  |  Classification: Operational - Control Room

=====================================================================
GENERAL NOTES
=====================================================================

Covers Electric Submersible Pump (ESP) and injection/production wellhead
alarms. All ESD actions are fail-safe. Acknowledge alarms on the HMI and
verify against redundant instrumentation before manual action.

=====================================================================
SOP-051-A : ESP MOTOR TEMPERATURE VERY HIGH
=====================================================================

Alarm Tag:        TAHH-ESP
Equipment:        Electric Submersible Pump (ESP) Motor
Trip Condition:   Motor temperature greater than 220 degrees C
ESD Level:        Level 3 (Well)

Required Action:
  1. Acknowledge the TAHH-ESP alarm on the HMI.
  2. Trip the ESP.
  3. Close the well shutdown valve SDV-201.
  4. Allow the motor to cool before any restart attempt.
  5. Notify the shift supervisor and log the event.

=====================================================================
SOP-051-B : ESP INTAKE PRESSURE VERY LOW
=====================================================================

Alarm Tag:        PALL-ESP
Equipment:        Electric Submersible Pump (ESP) Intake
Trip Condition:   Intake pressure less than 500 kPag
ESD Level:        Level 3 (Well)

Required Action:
  1. Acknowledge the PALL-ESP alarm on the HMI.
  2. Trip the ESP to prevent pump-off and gas locking.
  3. Raise the low-intake-pressure alarm to the shift supervisor.
  4. Investigate reservoir inflow before restart.

=====================================================================
SOP-051-C : INJECTION WELLHEAD TEMPERATURE VERY LOW
=====================================================================

Alarm Tag:        TALL-INJ
Equipment:        Injection Wellhead
Trip Condition:   Temperature less than 100 degrees C
ESD Level:        Level 3 (Well)

Required Action:
  1. Acknowledge the TALL-INJ alarm on the HMI.
  2. Close the injection shutdown valve SDV-101.
  3. Raise the low-temperature alarm to the shift supervisor.
  4. Investigate steam supply loss to the injection wellhead.

=====================================================================
SOP-051-D : PRODUCTION WELLHEAD PRESSURE VERY HIGH
=====================================================================

Alarm Tag:        PAHH-PROD
Equipment:        Production Wellhead
Trip Condition:   Wellhead pressure greater than 3,500 kPag
ESD Level:        Level 3 (Well)

Required Action:
  1. Acknowledge the PAHH-PROD alarm on the HMI.
  2. Close the production wing valve SDV-301.
  3. Confirm wellhead pressure begins to fall.
  4. Notify the shift supervisor and log the event.
  5. Do not reopen until the overpressure cause is identified.
